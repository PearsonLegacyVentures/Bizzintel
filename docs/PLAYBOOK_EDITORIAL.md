# BizzIntel editorial standard — founder-led, evidence-first

## What is sourced from Amar's previously discussed approach

- Clarify the idea and customer problem before investing in features.
- Problem → root cause → solution → customer segments → pain points → benefits → market opportunity → jobs-to-be-done → importance/dissatisfaction → opportunity map → decision (TEST / BUILD / PARK / KILL; SCALE only with established results).
- Visualize early with a simple prototype, including Lovable when appropriate, instead of fully building an untested product.
- Establish a credible digital identity: domain, website or simple landing page, custom email, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn where relevant, business contact methods.
- Confirm real Google Business Profile eligibility rather than assuming every online venture qualifies.
- Test genuine interest via direct outreach or measured Meta ads; track qualified enquiries, not vanity reach.
- Pitch to the actual audience, with a story, defensible numbers, a clear ask and a 90-second explanation.
- Use HOOK → INFO → CTA for social and marketing copy.
- Track grants, accelerators, cohorts, tenders and partner opportunities weekly, with *verified open / announced-review / closed / watchlist* status categories.

## Important qualification

The exact wording, examples, ordering and instructional methods in \`src/playbooks.js\` are editorial implementations of those discussed ideas. They have **not** all been explicitly approved or tested by the founder. Do not invent successes, testimonials, company metrics or cite illustrative examples as actual user evidence. Founder review is required before making marketing claims about a proprietary verified method.

## Lesson contract

Every step uses a stable \`id\` (never derive saved progress solely from array position) and contains:
- clear name and problem addressed
- a sequence of actionable how-to instructions
- a tangible output
- an illustrative example, plainly labeled
- a common mistake
- expected evidence
- optional copyable prompt

Source code: \`src/playbooks.js\`. Completion key: \`pb:<module-id>:<step-id>\`. Workbook key: \`pb-note:<module-id>:<step-id>\`. Older index-based completion is read for compatibility; do not silently delete existing learner work when editing.

## Release gate

- Review Module 1 (12 steps) and Module 2 (10 steps) with Amar before describing them as his finalized method.
- Check current official platform docs (especially GBP and LinkedIn); links are references, not guarantees of eligibility.
- Test each module with people at different business stages.
- Add founder-approved walkthrough videos, screenshots, checklists and examples when available.
- Verify RLS and authentication with two separate users before collecting actual customer data.
- Do not publish imaginary grants, reviews, earnings or service guarantees.
